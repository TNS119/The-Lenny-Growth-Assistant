# PRODUCT.md - The Lenny Growth Assistant

## Platform
Web (Responsive desktop dual-pane with collapsible drawer on mobile/tablet)

## Target Audience
Product Managers, Growth Leaders, Heads of Product, and Early-Stage Founders. Users who read fast, value concrete metrics and citations, and need operational clarity without listening to 200+ hours of audio.

## Value Proposition
Instant, source-grounded synthesis of Lenny's Podcast transcript wisdom, paired with a Ship 30 for 30 writing engine and a Claude-style sandboxed artifact viewer.

## Surface Modes
- **Left Pane (Operate Mode):** High scanability, rapid task completion, session switching, and in-between model toggling.
- **Right Pane (Read / Experience Mode):** Distraction-free reading of 1,250-word strategic essays, syntax-highlighted code inspection, and interactive execution inside a secure sandbox.
